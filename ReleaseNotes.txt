1.0
- Initial release.
- Added ValidationBehavior: how much a validator reports, along two axes — across its properties
  (ValidationBehaviors.Class) and within one property's rule chain (ValidationBehaviors.Property).
  Configurable on the registration, on a validator, and per chain with WithValidationBehavior.
  Replaces ContinueOnFailure(), which is equivalent to WithValidationBehavior(ValidationBehavior.All).
  The defaults are unchanged behaviour: every property, one message each.
